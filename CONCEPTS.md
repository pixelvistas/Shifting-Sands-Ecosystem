# Concepts and acceptance criteria

What each prototype must let a participant see or do, without being told.
Checked on the sandbox; the headless previews only show that the
mechanism exists. (Prototype 4's criteria are in this file on
`prototype/4-scene`.)

## Prototype 6 ("Lenia lab", branch `prototype/6-lenia`)

### Step 1: organisms on their own

Display mode "Lenia lab": Lenia organisms alone on a black background; no
terrain, water, succession or plants. The mouse stands in for a hand.

A participant can see, without being told:

1. that small creatures move on their own;
2. that they keep their shape as they move;
3. that touching them disturbs or destroys them.

Checked on the sand with the erase brush as the "touch" until the hand is
connected; a participant watches from about 1 m.

### Next steps (not built yet)

- Step 2: the touch from the Kinect (a hand or a finger in the sand erases
  or pushes matter) instead of the mouse.
- Step 3: the mask from the water simulation (organisms live in the water)
