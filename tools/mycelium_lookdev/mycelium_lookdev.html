Mycorrhiza Look-Dev

Drag across the field to sweep the sand. Wherever you sweep lights up at once, with streaks running along your stroke; fresh light is cyan and warms to amber within seconds, and it stays. Over time a fine mesh of pale hyphae grows across the dark sand between the lit areas, with light pulsing along it.

–

What is stand-in here: the sand is generated, your pointer plays the role of a hand seen by the Kinect, and the colour warms with time since the ground was touched. In the sandbox, warming follows the succession model's recovery of that ground instead. About 1 px is 1 mm of sand.
